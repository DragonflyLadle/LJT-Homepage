## Research Interests and Skills

- Natural language processing and machine learning.
- LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning.
- Hallucination in vision-language models.
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability.
- Verifiable reasoning-data synthesis at scale.
- Vision-language chart understanding and perception-bottleneck analysis.
- Truthfulness hyperplane and inner-representation analysis.
- Hallucination alerting and mitigation from internal model representations.

- Composition of parameter-efficient modules using arithmetic operations.
